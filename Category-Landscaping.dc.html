A wild,
often native,
and overall
ecological
approach
to
landscapes

Our goals are, in no particular order:

  • Native plants, as many as possible, with permission for others that are well-adapted, supportive of life, or simply joyful plants (that are neither bullies nor invasive)
  • To find where modern needs are served with respect to natural patterns
  • Inner and outer biome health
  • Biodiversity and more support for pollinators, birds, and wildlife
  • More butterflies, more access to the magic that is naturally available to us
  • Working with the environment instead of against it
  • Designing to avoid chemical use
  • Designing for more ease, less maintenance, and minimal resource usage
  • To generate more joy in our immediate environments
  • To enjoy collaborating with all types of uniquely brilliant folks, and lastly,
  • To minimize waste in every step of the process.
