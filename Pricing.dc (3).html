Pricing

Haskins Design & Build is a landscape design / build firm that offers design and installation with maintenance guidance, and often referrals for local maintenance partners. With any new custom landscape project, it starts with a conversation - reach out via email to share what you have in mind!

You can then book a Design Consultation if a site visit or an hour-long video call is the next best step. If we decide to continue from there, we'll get into the Landscape Design and Layout Plan with Estimate territory. Budgets for our custom landscape installations start at a $15,000 minimum and go up from there depending on size, client preferences, and site factors.

Design Consultation

$250 flat rate

  • One hour on-site consultation.
  • I'm happy to consult with anyone - there's no project minimum for these design consultations.
  • Details, referrals, and ideas discussed will be shared following the consultation.
  • Consultation fee is paid up-front and is separate from other design and/or installation work. All phases of work require their own special amount of effort and resources.

Landscape Design and Layout Plan with Estimate

$150 an hour

  • Concept plans (sketch on site survey) with layout of landscape, hardscape, and location of design elements – average time for a ¼ acre landscape is 3-5 hours.
  • Planting plans (photo-realistic sketch of plants at maturity) showing the location, name, size, and quantity of plants – average time for a planting plan for a ¼ acre landscape is 5-10 hours after concept plan is approved.
  • Note: planting plans are incredibly time-sensitive. These plans aren't built simply from what works here, they're built with what is actually possible to purchase in the size and quantity needed for your project, and nursery availabilities fluctuate a lot from one week to the next.
  • I can offer alternatives for each plant in case the preferred one is no longer available, but either keep the planting plan work reserved for when you're ready to plant, or you can incur additional hours for a redesign if plant availabilities have shifted significantly after substantial time between design and planting.
  • The design fees associated with this phase are separate from other design and/or installation work. All phases of work require their own special amount of effort and resources.
