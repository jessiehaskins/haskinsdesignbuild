Rainwater Catchment Tank + System Installations

All tanks shown here were manufactured by Metal Rain Tanks, LLC.

I haven't installed a rain catchment tank in years but I can't think of one that didn't feel like an important, useful thing to do. Rainwater is the absolute best for plants and a barrel really doesn't suffice in our long summers, and something about keeping water on hand provides a sense of security - especially if it's dry for a while or there's a big storm or plumbing leak that affects the water supply.

Often we had a ton of rain in Houston, TX so it's easy to take for granted but more than a few times I've had to fill the tub or find water to flush after a big storm, and droughts are no joke when it's a big one. Add a filtration system and connect it to the house, shop, barndominium, etc for use inside or to an automated irrigation system or just a simple hose that's gravity-fed, and that's one natural resource you can keep in abundance regardless of the state of the world.

The brilliant, funny folks who made these tanks - a master plumber that I was incredibly fortunate to learn a from (and be saved by on a few hairy projects), and her engineer-brained partner who fabricated machines in their large shop to make these tanks happen - have since retired, but there are other folks in the tank industry that I'm sure you could call on, probably based in Austin, TX.
