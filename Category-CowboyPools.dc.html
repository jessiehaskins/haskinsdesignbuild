Cowboy Pools

In February of 2023, I was hired to open a Houston location for my friends at Cowboy Pools in Austin. I get to see a lot of Cowboy Pool installations with this job now and have genuinely fallen in love with this product. Not only are they cute, they're a very practical and economical alternative to a whole in-ground pool. I'm grateful to get to install with the methods and materials honed over 4+ years of the busy Austin shop perfecting the craft - the way we build them out continues to stand the test of time. They're surprisingly easy to maintain, too. 10/10 would install again. Here are some of my favorites, and I did not install any of the landscapes surrounding them. If you're interested in more info, check out their website at cowboypools.com.
