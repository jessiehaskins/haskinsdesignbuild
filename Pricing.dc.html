Pricing

Haskins Design & Build is a landscape design / build firm that offers design and installation with maintenance guidance, and often referrals for local maintenance partners. Before a new custom landscape project begins, the client has a choice of two different types of consultations. Budgets for our custom landscapes start at $30,000 minimum and go up from there depending on size, client preferences and site factors.

Design Consultation

$250 flat rate

  • One hour on-site consultation.
  • Details, referrals, and ideas discussed will be shared following the consultation.
  • Consultation fee is paid up-front and applied to the cost of a landscape design and/or installation project.

Landscape Design and Layout Plan with Estimate

$150 an hour

  • Concept plans (sketch on site survey) with layout of landscape, hardscape, and location of design elements – average time for a ¼ acre landscape is 3-5 hours.
  • Planting plans (photo-realistic sketch of plants at maturity) showing the location, name, size, and quantity of plants – average time for a planting plan for a ¼ acre landscape is 5-10 hours after concept plan is approved.
